ContentFlow

A personal publishing desk that runs on its owner's own computer.

ContentFlow plans, prepares and publishes content for a small set of brands run by one person. It is not a public service: there are no sign-ups, and it only ever acts on accounts its owner connects to it.

What it does with YouTube

When its owner connects a YouTube channel, ContentFlow uploads the videos the owner has prepared and approved, and reads that channel's own statistics (views, likes, comments, watch time) so the owner can see how each video is doing. Nothing else.

Everything ContentFlow stores stays on the owner's computer. Read the privacy policy for the details.
